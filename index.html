Welcome to VSPS

A Legacy of Learning, A Future of Possibilities

Vihasant Sagar Public School, located in the heritage city of Agra, has been a beacon of academic excellence for over two decades. We are committed to delivering a holistic education that combines rigorous academics with character development, creativity, and physical well-being.

Our state-of-the-art campus, dedicated faculty, and child-centric approach prepare every student to face the world with confidence and compassion.

Why Us

Why Choose Vihasant Sagar?

Academic Excellence

A rigorous CBSE curriculum delivered by experienced educators, with consistently outstanding board results.

Modern Facilities

Smart classrooms, well-equipped science & computer labs, library, and sports infrastructure.

Dedicated Faculty

A team of passionate and qualified teachers who mentor students academically and personally.

Sports & Wellness

Football, cricket, basketball, athletics, and yoga — a balanced program for body and mind.

Arts & Culture

Music, dance, drama, painting and an annual cultural festival to nurture creative talent.

Safe Environment

CCTV-monitored campus, trained staff, and a strict anti-bullying policy ensure student safety.
