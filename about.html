Our Story

A Journey of Excellence Since 1998

Founded in 1998 by a group of visionary educators, Vihasant Sagar Public School began with a humble batch of 80 students. Today, we are home to over 2000 students and are recognised as one of the most trusted CBSE schools in Agra.

Over two decades, we have evolved while staying true to our founding belief: education should empower, inspire and transform. Our sprawling green campus, modern infrastructure and time-tested values continue to make VSPS a preferred choice for parents.

We believe in inclusive learning where every student — regardless of background — finds opportunities to discover their potential.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution where students discover their unique strengths, develop a strong moral compass, and emerge as compassionate global citizens prepared to lead with integrity in a complex, ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To provide a stimulating, nurturing and student-centric learning environment that develops academic rigour, creative thinking, physical wellness, and life skills — preparing children for the demands of higher education and beyond.
