Curriculum

Learning that Inspires

At VSPS, we follow the CBSE curriculum, enriched with experiential learning, life skills, and a wide range of co-curricular pursuits. Our academic structure is divided into four phases — each carefully designed to match the developmental stage of the learner.

Nursery - KG

Pre-Primary

Play-based learning, phonics, numeracy, motor skills and storytelling for ages 3-5.

Class I - V

Primary

Foundational literacy, numeracy, science exploration and creative arts in a joyful environment.

Class VI - VIII

Middle School

Concept-driven learning across Sciences, Humanities, Mathematics and Languages with project work.

Class IX - XII

Senior School

Streams in Science, Commerce & Humanities with rigorous board prep and career guidance.

Class XI - XII

Streams Offered

Science

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Biology, English, plus optional Computer Science / Physical Education.

Career Paths: Engineering, Medicine, Research, Data Science

Commerce

Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English, plus Mathematics / Informatics Practices.

Career Paths: CA, MBA, Finance, Entrepreneurship

Humanities

History, Political Science, Geography, Economics, English, with options like Psychology / Sociology.

Career Paths: Civil Services, Law, Media, Social Sciences
