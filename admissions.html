Required

Documents Checklist

  • Birth certificate (photocopy)
  • Previous school report card / TC (for transfers)
  • Aadhaar card of student & parents
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Address proof (utility bill / passport)
  • Medical fitness certificate
